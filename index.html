How to Play

Destroy every enemy Command Center and production building (Barracks, War Factory, Drone Bay, Airfield). Metal comes from your Command Center and Oil Derricks (dark oil fields); energy from Power Plants. Production streams resources: everything you build drains metal and energy continuously, and if either runs short all production slows down. Build Barracks → War Factory → Drone Bay / Tech Lab / Airfield. The Tech Lab unlocks Mk II units and the Advanced Tech Lab Mk III (use the Mk I/II/III selector or press T). Drones (robots, EW dogs, FPV swarms, deep-strike carriers) are cheap but vulnerable to electronic warfare: EW dogs, trucks, aircraft and towers slow and blind enemy drones and aircraft radar and break missile locks. Units beyond your vision are hidden by the fog of war – use scouts and the UAV.

Units

Infantry → cheap, good vs infantry. AT teams, IFVs and tanks beat vehicles. Heavy armor shrugs off bullets but fears AT missiles and helicopters. Anti-air (MANPADS, Mobile AA, SAM) counters aircraft. Artillery, rocket launchers and ballistic missiles need spotters (vision) and are deadly against buildings.
